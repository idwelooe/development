Privacy Policy

Obwongobwo is designed to work offline and respect your privacy.

The app does not require an account or login, does not collect personal data, and does not request device permissions.

01. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Obwongobwo, a math challenge and quiz game developed by IDWELOOE. It explains what information the app handles and how the app is designed to protect user privacy.

No account requiredYou can play without signing up, logging in, or providing a name or email address.
Offline gameplayThe core game is designed to function without an internet connection.
No personal data collectionObwongobwo does not collect or transmit personal information to IDWELOOE.
No permission requestsThe app does not require access to your camera, contacts, location, microphone, or similar device features.

02. Information we collect

IDWELOOE does not collect personal information through Obwongobwo. The app does not ask you to submit personal details and does not require user registration.

Obwongobwo is not designed to collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, audio, advertising identifiers, payment information, or other personally identifiable information.

03. Local game data

To provide game features such as progress, XP, levels, best scores, preferences, or similar gameplay state, Obwongobwo may store information locally on your device. This information is used to provide your game experience and is not transmitted to IDWELOOE.

Because this information stays on the device, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app may remove locally saved progress.

04. Internet, accounts and permissions

Obwongobwo's gameplay is designed to work offline. No account, sign-up, or login is required. The app is also designed not to request sensitive device permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or files for its normal operation.

05. Advertising and third-party tracking

Obwongobwo does not use your personal information for targeted advertising or profiling. The app does not intentionally include third-party tracking for the purpose of collecting personal user data.

06. Data sharing and sale

Because Obwongobwo does not collect personal information, IDWELOOE does not sell or share personal information collected through the app with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

07. Users aged 13 and above

Obwongobwo is intended for individuals aged 13 years and above. The app is educational in nature and is designed without account registration or intentional collection of personal information.

If you believe personal information has nevertheless been provided to or received by IDWELOOE in connection with the app, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

08. Security

Obwongobwo minimizes privacy risks by avoiding unnecessary collection and transmission of personal information. Game progress that is stored locally is also subject to the security protections of your device and operating system.

09. Changes to this policy

IDWELOOE may update this Privacy Policy if Obwongobwo's features or privacy practices change. When the policy is updated, the effective date shown at the top of this page will be revised. Material changes should be reflected in the privacy information made available with the app.
